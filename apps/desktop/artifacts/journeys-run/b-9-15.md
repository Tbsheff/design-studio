| 5 | Medium | There is no canvas-frame tidy in the UI (no frame multi-select, no command). Arrow nudge does not move in-flow layers, and no hint shows. |
| 6 | Medium | The MCP schemas fight the agent. `insert_jsx` asks for a `targetSource` parameter that does not exist. `create_comment` reveals its required keys one at a time. `get_layers` has no text search. The `export` and `create_design_rule` descriptions do not match their behavior. |
