Questions, answered

What counts as an event?

One event is one tracked action, such as a page view, a click, or a custom signup event. Sessions and users are calculated from events and are not billed separately.

What happens if I go over my limit?

We keep collecting. You get an email at 80% and again at 100%, then a month to upgrade or trim your tracking before anything is paused.

Can I change plans later?

Yes, at any time. Upgrades apply immediately and are prorated. Downgrades take effect at the next billing date.

Do you offer refunds?

Monthly plans can be cancelled whenever you like. If you pay yearly and change your mind within 30 days, we refund the full amount.

Is the free trial really free?

Yes. The 14-day trial has no card requirement and includes every Team feature, so you can test funnels before you choose.

Where is my data stored?

You pick a US or EU region when you create a project. Data stays in that region and you can export or delete it at any time.
