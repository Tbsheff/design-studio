How a bag reaches you

Four stops, and none of them is a shelf.

Most coffee sits for weeks before it reaches you. We built the whole route around the window when beans taste their best: days 4 to 21 after roast.

  1. You tell us how you brew

    Pour-over, espresso, French press or drip. We grind to match, or ship whole bean if you own a grinder.

    2 minutes

  2. We pick the lot for you

    Our green buyer, Ana Restrepo, tastes about 300 samples a quarter. Ten of them make it into a Kiln box.

    Every month

  3. We roast your bag to order

    Small 15 kg batches on Tuesday and Thursday. Each bag carries its roast date and the name of the farm.

    Tue & Thu

  4. It ships the same afternoon

    A recyclable, letterbox-sized pouch with a one-way valve. No signature, no waiting at home.

    Within 72 h
